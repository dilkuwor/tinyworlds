🌧️ Rain on the Glass

A rainy window looking out over a soft-focus city. Drops gather, stall, slide and merge, leaving clear trails through the mist. Wipe a space to look out; the glass slowly fogs over again.

Things to try
Wipe Drag across the glass to clear the fog and smear the drops away.
Add a drop Click or tap the glass. A fat drop forms and slides down.
Squeegee Wipe the whole window clean in one sweep.
Storm Switch the time to Storm for heavy rain, lightning and distant thunder.
