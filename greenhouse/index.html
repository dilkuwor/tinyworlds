    🪴 The Greenhouse

    A painted Victorian glasshouse: a cedar potting bench, a back shelf and a hanging basket, with seven plants of your own. They live on real time.

    How it works: soil dries over about a day, and thirsty plants droop (they never die). Plants grow only while their soil is moist, so the care you give today shows tomorrow: new leaves unfurl, vines trail further, the fern sends up fronds, and the orchid grows a flower spike and opens its buds one by one. When you come back, the greenhouse tells you what changed.

    Things to try
    WaterAim the can over a pot and hold. Watch the soil darken and a droopy plant slowly lift.
    MistSwitch to the mister and spray the leaves; it beads them with dew and keeps them moist.
    Check on themHover any plant to see its moisture, age and growth stage.
    VentClick the roof pane to let in a breeze.
