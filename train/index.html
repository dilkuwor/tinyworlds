🚆 Night Train

A window seat on a night train. The world slides past in layers: far hills and town lights barely move, farmhouses glide, hedges hurry and telegraph poles flash by. Now and then a tunnel swallows the view and you catch your own carriage reflected in the glass, a station drifts past, or another train rushes by. This is the one you leave running.
