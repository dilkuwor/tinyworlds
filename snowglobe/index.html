❄️ The Snow Globe

A glass globe on a wooden music box, with a small snowy village inside. Pick it up and shake it, then put it down and watch the snow settle on the roofs. The sky inside follows your own clock.

Things to try
❄️ Shake itDrag the globe around and let go. On a phone, shake the phone.
🎵 The keyWind the music box. The train runs while it plays, and both slow down as it unwinds.
🏠 The cottageTap it to turn the lights on or off. They come on by themselves after dark.
✨ GlitterA few flakes catch the light as they turn.
