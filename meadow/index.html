✨ Night Meadow

Tall grass under the stars, a pond holding the moon, and a meadow full of fireflies. Each one blinks on its own clock, but every flash nudges its neighbours, so if you leave them be, the whole meadow slowly learns to blink together, just like real synchronous fireflies.

Things to try
Hold stillRest the pointer in the meadow and fireflies drift over to circle it.
CallClick or tap to raise a lantern; fireflies come from all over.
HarmonyWatch the meter climb as the meadow falls into step.
Look upNow and then, a shooting star.
