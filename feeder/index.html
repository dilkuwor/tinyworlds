🐦 The Feeder

A garden feeder outside the window. Scatter seed and see who comes: they fly in, perch, peck and hop, sing a little, and leave when they are full. Move gently; sudden movement sends the whole flock up and away, though they always drift back.

Visitors
House sparrowYour most regular guest, happy on the lawn.
GoldfinchBright yellow, prefers the feeder ports.
ChickadeeTiny and bold; sings “fee-bee”.
CardinalA rarer red visitor, often at golden hour.
Blue jayBig and bossy; small birds make way.
RobinMost likely in winter, hopping in the snow.
