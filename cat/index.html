🐈 Ragdoll Cat Guide

Meet a fluffy seal-point Ragdoll. Gentle and floppy, it watches your cursor, stalks and pounces, loves treats, and purrs when you pet it. Leave it alone for a while and it will curl up in its bed for a nap.

Ways to play
Pet Move slowly over the cat. It leans in and purrs. Scrub too fast and it walks off.
Treats Click the floor or press Space. It trots over and munches.
Yarn ball Click to toss it. The cat bats it around the room.
Laser A red dot follows your pointer. Watch the crouch, wiggle and pounce.
