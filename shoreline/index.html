🌊 The Shoreline

A quiet beach seen from above. Waves wash up the sand and slide back. Draw or write something near the water, and watch the next wave soften it and the one after take it away.

Things to try
✍️ DrawDrag across the sand with your finger or mouse.
💬 WriteType a word and it's written by the water's edge.
🐚 ShellsWaves leave them behind. Tap one to keep it.
🦀 The crabIt wanders the wet sand, runs from your hand, and digs in when the water comes.
🌊 Big waveEvery few waves one reaches further. You can also call one.
🌙 Tide and timeThe light follows your clock, and the tide rises and falls through the day.
